FOR DONORS

Every Donation Starts a Chain of Care

Your donation can become part of a coordinated blood network connecting donations with organizations and patients who need blood.

Stage 01

DONOR

Voluntary donor profile registered within the regional coordination network.

Stage 02

DONATION

Collection event recorded at an accredited facility or mobile blood drive.

Stage 03

BLOOD UNIT

Discrete unit labeled, screened and assigned an operational tracking record.

Stage 04

INVENTORY

Maintained in cold storage and discoverable for regional hospital needs.

Stage 05

PATIENT / REQUEST

Controlled reservation and issuing fulfilling clinical doctor orders.

Medical Safety Principle (§6)

This platform is an operational and inventory-management coordination system, not a clinical decision engine. It does not autonomously determine donor eligibility, transfusion appropriateness, or cross-matching compatibility. All clinical judgment is strictly reserved for certified healthcare practitioners and accredited blood transfusion facilities.
